# Presentation Talking Points

## 30-second explanation
"Our original proposal focused on Numa AI as the communication solution for A to Z Power Washing. We took inspiration from Numa's automated customer-communication approach and created our own version specifically for A to Z. Our implementation handles customer questions, quote intake, job details and photos, scheduling requests, lead tracking, conversation history, and employee follow-up in one system."

## What to show
- Customer Assistant
- Quote & Scheduling
- Owner Dashboard
- Lead Details
- Appointments
- Conversations
- Reports

## What to say about the AI
"The assistant automates approved, repetitive customer interactions and routes uncertain or sensitive situations to a human. We intentionally prevent it from inventing prices or confirming appointments without employee review."

## What to say about Numa
"Numa was the inspiration and starting point for the communication model. The final application is our own implementation customized for A to Z Power Washing; it is not Numa's proprietary code."

## What to say about the database
"We used SQLite and SQL to centralize leads, messages, appointment requests, photos, and employee notes. That solves the proposal's problem of customer information being spread across separate communication channels."

## If asked why the implementation changed
"After the proposal stage, we decided that building a customized version would let us demonstrate the workflow directly and tailor the system to A to Z's exact needs. We kept the main communication concepts from our Numa research while implementing the software ourselves."

## Do not claim
- That the team owns or modified Numa's proprietary software.
- That live SMS/phone service is connected when it is not.
- That an appointment is confirmed before staff confirmation.
- That the system generated real business performance improvements unless real measured data exists.
